Added the `:control_master_policy` option for `control_master: :auto`. Reusing a ControlMaster skips key exchange, host key verification and authentication, so explicitly passed master-owned options (`:verify_host_key`, `:known_hosts`, `:host_key`, `:kex`, `:encryption`, `:hmac`, `:pubkey_algorithms`, `:keys`, `:key_data`, `:password`, `:passphrase`, `:auth_methods`, `:use_agent`, ...) and a ControlPath without `%C` or `%h`/`%n`, `%p`, `%r` tokens can't be verified on the proxied session. `:allow` (default) reuses and logs them as before, `:direct` bypasses the master for a fresh direct connection, and `:strict` raises `Net::SSH::ControlMasterSecurityError` before any channel is opened or remote command sent; options that only shape the channel layer keep working over the master [#990]
