Added a destination policy for port forwarding, set with Net::SSH.start's `forward_policy:` option or `Forward#policy=`. It is called with a `Forward::Request` describing the direction (`:local`, `:remote` or a channel the server opens, `:server`), the destination host and port, the listener, the originator, and the session id and authenticated user when they can be observed, and may refuse by returning nil. Remote and server-opened destinations are resolved once when approved and the connection is pinned to those addresses; local forwarding is resolved by the server, so the policy limits the name and port only. Cancelling a forward revokes its permit, so a channel the server opens afterwards, or a local connection accepted after cancellation, is refused before it connects. Without a policy forwarding is unrestricted, as before
